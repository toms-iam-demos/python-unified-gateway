GENERIC DESIGN

A municipal team. A fictional workspace.

Original civic icon and abstract color treatment. No official seal, location photograph or real organization is represented. Reviewer personas are illustrative; this is a local simulation.

01

Keep existing systems in their lane.

A fictional sourcing portal and purchasing platform remain separate from agreement review. No real municipality’s systems are asserted.

02

Show one useful exception.

A fictional pricing discrepancy of $6,240 is more persuasive than a generic signature animation. Trace it to inputs, resolve it, then preserve the review.

03

Policy text needs an owner.

Dated procurement policies and changing legal conditions require confirmation. This demo makes no automatic award, legal-compliance, participation-goal or payment decision.
